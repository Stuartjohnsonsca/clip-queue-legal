Clip Queue

Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Clip Queue is a desktop tool that runs on one person's own computer. Its owner uses it to review short video clips they have made and post the approved ones to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It has no other users.

What it accesses

When the owner connects a TikTok account, Clip Queue receives, through TikTok's official Login Kit:

  • basic profile details (display name, username, avatar), used to show which account a post will go to;
  • permission to upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish), used only when the owner approves a specific clip.

It does not read messages, followers, likes, viewing history or any other TikTok data.

Where data is stored

Access tokens, clips, captions and the post log are stored only on the owner's computer. Clip Queue has no server and no database outside that computer, and it does not send data to anyone except the platform (TikTok or YouTube) where the owner chose to post.

Sharing and selling

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect at any time by deleting the local token file or revoking access in TikTok's settings under Security > Manage app permissions. Revoking access stops all further posting.

Children

Clip Queue is not intended for anyone under 18.
